Lancio is three contracts on Robinhood Chain, plus one token contract per launch. None of them is a proxy, so none can be upgraded. The source is verified on Blockscout: open any address below to read it.

## Lancio contracts

<ContractList group="lancio" />

Every token launched on Lancio is its own contract: a standard ERC-20 with 18 decimals and a fixed supply of 1,000,000,000, minted once. It has no owner and no mint or burn function. Its address is on the token's page.

## Uniswap v4 on Robinhood Chain

Graduated tokens trade in Uniswap v4 pools. These are Uniswap's own contracts, used as they are.

## Admin powers

The launchpad has an owner. Ownership can be handed over in two steps, but never renounced. The owner can do exactly two things:

- change the treasury address that receives Lancio's share of fees;
- pause and resume the creation of **new** tokens.

The owner cannot:

- stop or pause trading, on any curve or in any pool;
- change any parameter, such as supply, curve, fees, launch window or graduation threshold, for any token;
- touch the ETH on a curve, a creator's unclaimed fees, or a locked position;
- upgrade any contract.

The locker and the hook have no owner at all.

These guarantees hold at the contract level. They rely on Robinhood Chain itself, which is covered in [Risks](/docs/risks).

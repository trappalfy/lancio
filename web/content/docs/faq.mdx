## Launching

<QuestionList>

<Question q="What does it cost to launch a token?">

Gas only. There is no launch fee. If you also buy your own token in the launch transaction, that buy is a normal curve buy with the 1% fee.

</Question>

<Question q="Can a creator get tokens for free?">

No. There are no allocations of any kind. A creator who wants to hold their token buys it from the curve, at the curve's price, like everyone else. During the launch window, that buy counts against the creator's 1%.

</Question>

<Question q="Can I change the name, ticker or supply after launch?">

No. The name, ticker and supply are fixed in the token contract. The rules of the curve, the window, graduation and fees are fixed for every token.

</Question>

</QuestionList>

## Buying and selling

<QuestionList>

<Question q="Why can't I buy more than 1% right after a launch?">

For the first two blocks, about 0.2 seconds, no wallet can buy more than 1% of the supply in total. See [Launch window](/docs/launch-window).

</Question>

<Question q="My buy from a smart-contract wallet failed right after launch. Why?">

During the launch window only regular wallets can buy; contracts cannot, and smart-contract wallets count as contracts. Try again once the window has passed.

</Question>

<Question q="Can I sell during the launch window?">

Yes. The window limits buys only. Selling back to the curve is always possible until the token graduates.

</Question>

<Question q="What does a price like 0.0₇393 ETH mean?">

The small number counts the zeros after the decimal point. 0.0₇393 ETH is 0.0000000393 ETH.

</Question>

<Question q="How is market cap calculated?">

Price times the full supply of 1,000,000,000, in ETH. Dollar values are converted at a recent ETH price and are approximate.

</Question>

</QuestionList>

## Graduation and the pool

<QuestionList>

<Question q="What happens to my tokens when a token graduates?">

Nothing changes in your wallet. It is the same token at the same address. Trading moves from the curve to the Uniswap v4 pool, which opens in the same transaction.

</Question>

<Question q="Can anyone remove the liquidity?">

No. The position belongs to the locker, which can only collect fees. It has no withdraw function, no owner, and cannot be upgraded. See [Liquidity lock](/docs/liquidity-lock).

</Question>

</QuestionList>

## Fees and rules

<QuestionList>

<Question q="How do creators claim their fees?">

From [Profile → Creator fees](/profile#creator-fees). Curve fees are credited with every trade. Pool fees are credited when someone collects them from the locker, and anyone can trigger that.

</Question>

<Question q="Can Lancio change the fees or the rules?">

No. Every parameter is fixed in the contracts. The owner can only change the treasury address and pause the creation of new tokens. See [Admin powers](/docs/contracts#admin-powers).

</Question>

<Question q="Can Lancio stop me from selling?">

No contract function can pause trading. Robinhood Chain's operator runs the sequencer and can delay or filter transactions; see [Risks](/docs/risks).

</Question>
